    How to Play

    • Hemmi runs by himself. You help him jump and spin.
    • Jump: tap the Jump button, tap the top of the screen, or press Space, W or the Up arrow. Hold for a higher jump. Jump again in the air for a double jump.
    • Mischief Spin: tap Spin, or press X, D or the Right arrow. Spin into crabs to bonk them silly, and spin into coconut stacks, crates and beach balls for big mischief points.
    • Avoid: rocks, spiky urchins, falling coconuts, lava cracks and lava bubbles. You have 3 hearts.
    • Collect: fruit for points, and golden star shards to buy outfits in the Wardrobe.
    • Pause: press P or Escape, or tap the pause button.
    • Need help? Turn on Assist Mode in Settings for slower speed or no damage.
