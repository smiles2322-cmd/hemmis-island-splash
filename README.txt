HEMMI'S ISLAND SPLASH  (version 1)
==================================
Crash-land. Cause chaos. Find your family.

Hemmi is a round, fuzzy little blue alien with teal cheeks and feet, a mint-green
star on his belly, glowing golden freckles, two springy antennae tipped with
glowing stars, and a curly spiral tail. His star pod crash-lands on sunny
Sunnyshell Island, where he causes lovable chaos and becomes best friends with
Teo, a boy who runs a fruit stand with his Grandma Rosa.

HOW TO OPEN IT
- On a computer: unzip, then open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.
  (Opening the file directly works for playing. Installing to a phone home
  screen needs the game hosted on a web link first.)
- On a phone: the game must be put on a web link. Then open the link and choose
  "Add to Home Screen". It works offline after the first visit.

HOW TO PLAY
- Hemmi runs by himself. Jump: tap Jump, tap the top of the screen, or press
  Space / W / Up arrow. Hold for a higher jump; jump again in the air for a
  double jump.
- Mischief Spin: tap Spin, or press X / D / Right arrow. Bonks crabs and knocks
  over coconut stacks, crates and beach balls for bonus points.
- Pause: P or Escape, or the pause button.
- Collect fruit for points and star shards to buy outfits in the Wardrobe.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- 3 levels: Splashdown Beach (with a wave-surfing stretch), Whispering Jungle,
  Rumble Volcano. Plus Endless Island Tour (beach, jungle, harbor town, volcano).
- 5 illustrated story pages, unlocked as you play, replayable in the Story Book.
- 4 outfits bought with in-game star shards only: heart sunglasses, straw hat,
  flower garland, tiny surfboard.
- Settings: music, sound effects, screen shake, reduce motion, high contrast,
  large text, Assist (slower speed, no damage), hazard sound cues, touch buttons.
- Progress saves on the device (browser storage).

NO real money, ads, accounts, chat, or data collection. Nothing is sent anywhere.
Music and sound effects are generated live in the browser.
